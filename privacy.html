Privacy Policy

DailyStateUpdates is a personal tool that uploads news videos to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

The app only uses the YouTube upload permission, granted by the channel owner, to upload videos to that channel. It does not read, collect, or store personal data from any other users.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Google policies

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.
